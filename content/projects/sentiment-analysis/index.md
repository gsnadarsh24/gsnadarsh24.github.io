Research project at CBIT Hyderabad on ensemble learning of transformer models for enhanced sentiment analysis.

## What I did

- Developed and fine-tuned an ensemble sentiment analysis pipeline using BERT, RoBERTa, XLNet, DistilBERT and ELECTRA.
- Implemented emoji de-emojization, advanced tokenization, regularization and AdamW optimization to improve generalization on the Sentiment140 and IMDB datasets.
- Applied a majority-voting ensemble strategy, achieving superior accuracy and robust performance across accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score and confusion-matrix evaluations.
