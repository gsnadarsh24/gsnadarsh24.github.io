A scheduler that gives the team early visibility into stuck and failed card loads without running scripts for each bank.

## What I built

- A Rails scheduler reporting card-loading status for all provider banks every 6 hours, replacing manual script checks.
- Tabular reports delivered by email to internal recipients managed through site configuration, with ~80 reports sent to date.
